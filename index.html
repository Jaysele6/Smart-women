About Us

Our mission is to help women become better, healthier, and more confident by understanding their bodies—one day at a time.

Who we are

Smart Woman is a women's health and wellness project created to support everyday women with practical tools and education. We believe every woman deserves clear, respectful information about her body—without shame, fear, or confusion.

Our goal

To empower women to recognise body changes early, track important health patterns, and make informed decisions. Whether it's menstrual cycle awareness, pregnancy support, or symptom tracking, we aim to make women feel supported and in control.

  • Build body awareness and confidence
  • Encourage healthy habits and self-care
  • Promote early action when something feels unusual

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This explains what we collect and how we use it.

Last updated: 2026-02-04

1) Information We Collect

  • Name (optional, for profile)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Health information (cycle, pregnancy, symptoms, and optional sex activity)
  • In-app messages and other user-generated content
  • Purchase history (subscriptions handled by Google Play Billing)
  • Device identifiers and diagnostics (used by Google services such as ads and billing)

2) How We Use Your Data

  • To provide app functionality (tracking, reminders, guidance)
  • To personalise your experience (based on your entries)
  • To process subscriptions and verify purchases via Google Play
  • To improve app stability and performance (crash logs and diagnostics)

3) Data Storage

Personal and health data you enter is stored locally on your device. We do not run our own user account system. You can remove your data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

4) Third-Party Services

Smart Woman uses Google services such as Google Play Billing and Google Mobile Ads. These services may collect device identifiers and usage data under their own privacy policies.

5) Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your personal or health entries with third parties, except for data required to operate Google services (billing/ads/diagnostics).

6) Security

Data transmitted off-device is encrypted in transit using secure protocols (HTTPS/TLS) where applicable.

7) Children's Privacy

Smart Woman is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
